# WOW-001 blind scoring evidence

On October 8, 2026, the repository owner approved sending the six synthetic,
arm-blind scorer packets to `opencode-go/glm-5.3` and
`opencode-go/kimi-k3`. Each packet contained its frozen workspace export,
second-agent answer, scenario key, rubric, and scorer instructions. It contained
no customer records, credentials, private first-agent transcript, or arm map.
`manifest.json` records the P1–P6 mapping only after both scorers finished.

`P*-glm.json` and `P*-kimi.json` preserve the two models' **initial** per-item
judgments and reasons. Kimi returned one complete 17-item sheet per packet.
GLM twice reached its output limit on P1 without score text. With a minimal
variant it then scored smaller item groups in fresh sessions, using the same
packet and frozen criteria. Two five-item groups also reached the output limit
without text and were retried as three-item and two-item groups. Those four
no-score attempts are listed in `manifest.json`; they are scorer execution
failures, not pilot-run invalidity. The manifest hashes the private raw JSONL;
the extracted initial sheets are the durable, credential-free evidence here.
Temporary scorer credential copies were removed after the runs.

`resolved.json` is generated by
`node scripts/wow-001/resolve-scores.mjs`. It keeps the initial sheets intact,
applies four recorded source-audit corrections against the frozen scenario
keys, and calculates the preregistered thresholds and matched-pair results.
One correction resolves a GLM/Kimi disagreement (P1 R10); three correct
over-credit by both scorers (P1 R6, P2 R5, P3 R8). The exact objects and key
requirements appear in the `overrides` section. The unblinded operator's
earlier sheet remains separately preserved in `score-operator-initial.md` and
was not treated as an independent blind score.

For the borderline S1 treatment R3 call, both independent scorers passed
`find_67bfd43a9081400e`. The finding says “Most likely cause” and “Inference,”
records confidence `0.85`, and states explicit Confirm and Refute tests while
noting that logs have not confirmed the explanation. We accept that as an
explicitly tentative, testable hypothesis under the frozen S1 key; the key
does not require the literal word “hypothesis.” If R3 were failed, S1 treatment
would still meet its 8/9 recording threshold. S2 and S3 would still make two
qualifying matched pairs.

All records in this directory describe a synthetic, one-machine pilot. They
do not establish Gate B, Gate C, ordinary team usage, or statistical
significance.
