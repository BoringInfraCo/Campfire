# SWH-001 participant packet — controlled comprehension task

You are asked to look at one workspace in the Campfire Viewer and answer seven
questions about what you find. This is a comprehension check of the Viewer, not
a test of you. Please work only from the Viewer for this task.

## Questions

Answer these in your own words, in this order. You may look around and inspect
details as much as you like before answering each one.

1. What is this workspace trying to do, and which work is open, in progress, or
   blocked?

2. Which work is yours or your agents', which belongs to a teammate or their
   agent, and which has no recorded owner?

3. What needs you, and what needs someone on the team?

4. What decisions are recorded, which need review or conflict, and what can the
   team safely treat as settled?

5. What record supports one important claim, and where would you inspect its
   artifact or code reference?

6. Which lists are complete, which are truncated, and what would you inspect
   next if an item is missing?

7. What would you do next, and what recorded facts make that safe?

## How the session runs

- The recorder opens a Viewer bound to you and shows you the workspace. Normal
  Viewer inspection (switching perspectives, opening details, scrolling,
  keyboard navigation) is allowed and encouraged.
- You have up to **15 minutes** for the seven questions.
- **Do not use the command line, MCP tools, or any other app** during the
  comprehension task. If you already know something about the workspace from
  outside the Viewer, say so instead of using it to answer.
- There is no prepared summary or answer key for you. The recorder will not
  coach you or confirm answers during the session.
- The recorder asks for your **verbatim** answers and may **time** the session.
  If you get a hint that gives away an answer, the recorder will note it.

## Perspective order

- **Participant 1:** open **My work** first, then move to **Team work**.
- **Participant 2:** open **Team work** first, then move to **My work**.

Please keep this order at the start of the session so the two sessions are
counterbalanced.
