# ATT-001 participant packet — controlled comprehension task

You are asked to look at one workspace in the Campfire Viewer and answer six questions about what you find. This is a comprehension check of the Viewer, not a test of you. Please work only from the Viewer for this task.

## Questions

Answer these in your own words, in this order. You may look around and inspect details as much as you like before answering each one. The six questions correspond, in order, to the six scored comprehension items.

1. Which of the work recorded here belongs to you or your agents, which belongs to a teammate or their agent, and which has no recorded assignee? Does the list an item appears in tell you whether you are allowed to update it?

2. Name one blocked task that someone should investigate — one recorded for you or one shown for the team. Give its source ID, its recorded assignee and status, and one safe next step to investigate it further.

3. Is any accepted decision marked as needing review? If so, which one is it, which citation makes it need review, and what has happened to its recorded acceptance?

4. The attention lists are built from the workspace read. Can you tell whether they are complete, or whether anything was truncated? What would a truncated list mean — and if a list is empty, what does that tell you about the team's remaining work?

5. Among the recorded decisions, what — if anything — can the team safely treat as settled, and what makes acting on a decision safe? Is any recorded rule superseded or otherwise no longer current, and may it be treated as live again?

6. When an item appears under team attention, what does that tell you about who will handle it? Does it mean a reviewer was assigned, an agent is currently running, a notification was sent, or you have permission to change something? Explain what the cue does and does not support.

## How the session runs

- The recorder opens a **Viewer bound to you** for the frozen workspace; each participant uses their own viewer, never a shared one. Normal Viewer inspection (switching between My work and Team work, opening details, scrolling, keyboard navigation) is allowed and encouraged.
- Work only from the Viewer: no command line, no MCP tools, no source code, no answer key, and no other app during the comprehension task. If you already know something about this workspace from outside the Viewer, say so instead of using it to answer.
- You have up to **15 minutes** for the six questions.
- The recorder preserves your **verbatim** answers and the read path (which records you inspected), and does not coach or confirm answers. If a hint gives away an answer, the recorder notes it, and the item is not asked again.
- Short notes for yourself are fine, but no external help: do not ask teammates, consult other documents or tools, or accept outside help.

## Perspective order

- **Participant P1:** open **My work** first, then move to **Team work**.
- **Participant P2:** open **Team work** first, then move to **My work**.

Please keep this order at the start of the session so the two sessions are counterbalanced. The recorder notes the order actually followed.
